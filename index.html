Built for Real Cities

Every technical decision serves two masters: the student explorer who just wants a fun walk, and the city GIS engineer who needs rigorous, standards-compliant data.

YOLOv8 Vision

Real-time object detection runs entirely on mobile hardware. The nano model achieves 30fps inference at under 3ms — no GPU, no cloud.

YOLOv8nCore MLTF LiteONNX

OGC Standards

WFS 2.0, CityGML 3.0, and GeoJSON output. Plug-and-play integration with Munich's existing GIS infrastructure — zero custom adapters needed.
