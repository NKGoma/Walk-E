Born in the Nymphenburg Palace. Married Franz Joseph. Escaped to Munich whenever she could. She loved the English Garden, hated the Viennese court, and rode horses through the city at dawn.

Franz Beckenbauer "Der Kaiser"

Born in Giesing, a working-class neighbourhood southeast of the city centre. Never forgot where he came from. Transformed German football from the streets of Munich to the World Cup stage. The city named a street after him.

Giesing birthplace Olympic Park Säbener Str.

Trade & Migration 70 min

Levi Strauss

Born in Buttenheim, Bavaria — just 60 km from Munich. Left with his family at 18, went to New York with nothing, then San Francisco. From a Bavarian Jewish household, he built the world's most recognisable garment. The jeans you're probably wearing right now.

Altstadt Viktualienmarkt Glockenbachviertel
"I left Bavaria with nothing. But Bavaria never left me."

Werner Heisenberg

Studied physics at the Ludwig Maximilian University here. Developed the uncertainty principle that changed how we understand reality. Later opposed the Nazi atomic bomb programme. A man of profound moral courage, born from Munich's academic tradition.

LMU Campus Deutsches Museum Englischer Garten
"The more precisely you observe one thing, the less you know about everything else."

The Experience

A Real Conversation.
Not a Tour Guide.

Walk-E uses AI to recreate each legend's voice, knowledge, and personality. Ask them anything. They answer as they would have — reacting to where you are, what you're seeing, what you want to know.

Voice-first conversation

Speak naturally. Walk-E listens through your headphones, understands context, and responds in the legend's voice — in real time as you walk.

Location-aware storytelling

The conversation shifts as you move. Walk past a specific gate and the story changes. Stop for a minute and they notice. Turn around and they ask why.

They remember you

Sisi remembers that you asked about her marriage last time. Freddie picks up the conversation where you left off. Each walk builds on the last.

Trees captured silently

While you talk, Walk-E's camera is quietly working. Trees are identified and logged in the background. You'll barely notice the notification.

The Hidden Mission

You Walk. Munich Gets Mapped.

Here's the quiet secret behind Walk-E. While you're deep in conversation with Freddie Mercury about Schwabing's music scene, the phone's camera is doing something remarkable.

Invisible to you

The camera runs silently. YOLO identifies every tree you pass. No interruption to your conversation. You never need to stop, tap, or aim.

Private by design

Photos never leave your device. Only species ID and GPS coordinates are transmitted — anonymously, in OGC-compliant format. GDPR Article 25, by design.

The 3-walk confirmation rule

One walker flags a tree. A second confirms it. A third locks it into Munich's official digital twin. No single point of failure, no accidental bad data.

The city gets better data

Munich's GeodataService receives a continuous stream of verified, OGC WFS 2.0-compliant tree data — updated by thousands of walkers who didn't even know they were contributing.
